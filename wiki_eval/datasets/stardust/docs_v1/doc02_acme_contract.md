# Acme Contract SV-14

Contract SV-14 names Acme Corporation as the developer of the Psionic Engine for the Skyvault Initiative. The contract requires Acme to deliver the PE-7 engine architecture, safety documentation, and integration support for Stardust Program probes.

Northstar Labs is listed as the supplier of Lumen Coil assemblies for the PE-7 engine. The Lumen Coil converts Helion Crystal output into a navigable psionic field.

The contract also appoints Dr. Mira Cole as technical lead and requires monthly reports to the Celestial Review Board.

Aliases used in the contract:

- Acme Corporation is also called ACME.
- The Skyvault Initiative is also called Skyvault.
- The Psionic Engine is sometimes abbreviated PE-7.
