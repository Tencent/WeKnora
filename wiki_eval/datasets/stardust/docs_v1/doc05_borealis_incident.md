# Borealis Station Incident Report

On 2032-05-09, Borealis Station recorded an unstable field event during a Stardust Program PE-7 test burn. The incident was traced to Lumen Coil batch LC-19 supplied by Northstar Labs.

No probe was lost, but the Stardust Program schedule was delayed by twelve days. Jonas Reed ordered a temporary suspension of test burns until Acme Corporation and Northstar Labs completed a joint review.

Dr. Mira Cole recommended replacing all LC-19 Lumen Coil assemblies before the next PE-7 test.
