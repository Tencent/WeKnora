# Borealis Station Overview

Borealis Station is a Skyvault Initiative test facility in Svalbard. It hosts the Stardust Program engine array and the PE-7 control room.

Jonas Reed manages Borealis Station field operations. The station stores spare Aurora Beacon modules, Helion Crystal shielding, and replacement Lumen Coil assemblies.

Borealis Station reports major incidents to the Celestial Review Board within 24 hours.
